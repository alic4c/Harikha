Welcome to Harikha Platform

Harikha is a smart app that helps you search for and find the owner of any vehicle to contact them when you need it moved or want to alert them about their car, using the plate number, with the option to call, message via WhatsApp, or send an in-app notification. The app also provides searching for and finding nearby parking spots and booking them electronically, saving time and effort and making parking easier and faster.

Frequently asked questions

Choose the appropriate category for you to view frequently asked questions related to the Harikha platform.

How do I search for a car?
You can search for a car by entering the plate number in the search field on the home page. You'll see the car's info and ways to contact its owner.
How do I add my car's info?
Tap "My Car Info" from the side menu, then enter the plate number and other car details. Others will be able to search for your car and contact you.
How do I book a parking spot?
Tap the parking icon on the home page, then choose a suitable spot on the map. You can see prices and distance before booking.
What's the difference between parking and overnight stay?
Parking: an hourly service for short stays.
Overnight: a full-night service at a fixed price.
How do I contact the car owner?
After searching for the car, you'll see contact options (call, WhatsApp, message) depending on the owner's settings.
How do I change contact settings?
From the side menu, choose "Contact Settings" then enable or disable the call and message options as you like.
Can I cancel a booking?
Yes, you can cancel according to the spot owner's policy. Cancellation fees may apply depending on timing.
How do I pay for parking?
Payment is made directly to the spot owner upon arrival. The app is only an intermediary for showing available spots.
Is my data safe?
Yes, we're committed to protecting your privacy. You can review the full privacy policy from the side menu.
How do I switch dark/light mode?
From the side menu, choose "App Appearance" then pick the mode you prefer.
How do I change the app color?
From the side menu, choose "App Appearance" then pick your favorite color from the available ones (blue, green, orange, pink, purple).
How do I register my garage on the platform?
Sign up as a parking owner through the app and add your garage details: location, capacity, prices, and working hours.
How do I receive booking requests?
Booking requests arrive as in-app notifications on the home page, and you can approve or review them from your dashboard.
Can I set my own prices and working hours?
Yes, you have full control over parking and overnight rates, working hours, and available services from your account.
How are bookings verified when vehicles enter?
Every confirmed booking issues an e-invoice with a unique barcode used to verify the booking and manage vehicle entry and exit.
How do I track my revenue?
Your dashboard provides detailed reports on bookings, occupancy, and revenue to help you grow your business.
